The Great Hylian Legend

Many thought this legend a sham,
It was just too much for their minds to cram,
And when the webs of evil were weaved,
That is when all believed.

Chaos spread everywhere,
To challenge the evil, no one would dare,
But in all this darkness there was one light,
And that light vowed, "I will fight."

The light purified all,
And to its power evil did fall,
Many thought this legend a mystery,
But now it has gone down in history.

What is this legend?
