The Hylian Legends Code

Serve the guild with honor, loyalty, humility, and dignity.

Our Guild Rules

  1. Represent the guild with respect.

    As a member of Hylian Legends, your actions reflect on the guild. Be kind and respectful to other players.

  2. Follow the game rules.

    Hacking, botting, scamming, stealing, or any other actions that could jeopardize the guild’s standing with CipSoft are not permitted.

  3. Ask for help, not gold.

    Do not ask fellow guild members for GP. If you need help obtaining an item, you may ask respectfully—but not repeatedly. We can work together through quests or help you earn the gold you need.

  4. Respect the guild’s property.

    Anyone who steals from the guild will have all their characters blacklisted from Hylian Legends.

  5. Keep Guild Chat open.

    Stay connected with your fellow guild members by keeping Guild Chat open while you play.

  6. Be a good guildmate.

    Be polite, friendly, helpful, honest, and kind. Scamming has no place in our guild.

  7. Thou shalt not steal!
