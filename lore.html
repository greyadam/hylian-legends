The Lore Library

The Triforce

Three sacred virtues. One enduring legend.

A golden emblem of three virtues, the Triforce stands at the heart of our Hylian-inspired identity. For Hylian Legends, its light is a reminder that strength means little without understanding, and that both need the courage to act.

A Sacred Legacy

In the legends of Hyrule, the Triforce is a sacred golden relic associated with the goddesses Din, Nayru and Farore. Its three pieces embody Power, Wisdom and Courage: distinct forces brought together in a single, unmistakable symbol.

Here in our guild's lore library, that symbol becomes a lens through which we tell our own story: not a claim to rule, but a commitment to stand together.

The Three Virtues

Power

The resolve to grow stronger, master our craft and protect the party. We measure strength not only by what we overcome, but by whom we help rise beside us.

Wisdom

The patience to learn, the judgment to prepare and the generosity to teach. Every shared route, careful plan and answered question strengthens the whole guild.

Courage

The heart to answer the call, face uncertainty and welcome a new companion. Courage is not the absence of fear; it is choosing not to leave others behind.

Strength in Balance

Our reading of the Triforce is one of balance. Power without wisdom can become recklessness. Wisdom without courage can remain only a plan. Courage without care can put the party at risk. Together, the three virtues give purpose to one another.

Three triangles make one emblem, just as different members make one guild. Each brings a different gift; none has to carry the journey alone.

The Hylian Legends Oath

For Hylian Legends, protecting the Triforce means honoring the ideals it inspires: train with purpose, share what you know and stand beside your companions when the road grows dark.

“We draw the blade together, or not at all.”

This is our guild's interpretation of the legend: a light carried forward through fellowship, one adventure at a time.
